REDLINER wiki - Weapons guide

REDLINER has many weapons, primarily guns and swords, with every one of them reserving their own use, playstyle and statistics. There is currently only one sword in the game.
The Redliner is the only melee weapon in the game, and serves as your primary means of fighting. The Redliner's stats are:
  • 20 damage
  • Infinite uses
  • Short range
The Redliner is the only way to start getting HEAT.
What is HEAT?
Usually initialised to "H", HEAT is the resource to build ammunition for your gun. In order to get HEAT, you must either land a hit with the Redliner, win a sword clash, or land a bullet with your gun. Once enough HEAT has been accumulated for that respective weapon (with varying HEAT requirements per weapon) a bullet is loaded and you can shoot your gun by pressing [Q]. A gun cannot accumulate more bullets than it can store (for example, the CASTIGATE can store 3 bullets, but the MONARCH can only store 1).
The CASTIGATE is your starting weapon, and has an appearance similar to a magnum revolver. Despite the fact that it is your first weapon, it is actually widely considered one of, if not THE best gun in the game. The CASTIGATE's stats are:
  • 60 damage
  • 3 bullets maximum loaded at once
  • Bullet cost: 100H
  • Draw time: 0.75s
  • Special ability: one handed weapon
The CASTIGATE is a light weapon, with the fastest draw time and lowest bullet cost of any gun in the game, leading to it often having a quick, annoying and spammy playstyle. It's special ability, onehandedness, means that you may use your grappling hook or wall run while the weapon is in use.
The PHOENIX is the second gun in the game, and it is available for purchase with 450,000 yen or 6 crimson credits. The PHOENIX's stats are:
  • 70 damage on AOE hit, 150 damage on direct hit
  • 1 bullet maximum loaded at once
  • Bullet cost: 150H
  • Draw time: 0.8s
  • Special ability: explosive bullets; projectile bullets
The PHOENIX is an interesting weapon, having a medium bullet cost, a draw time nearly as fast as the CASTIGATE and explosive bullets. The PHOENIX's bullets are projectile, meaning that instead of simply casting one large ray in the direction you are facing, the hitbox changes as the bullet moves. Before it was nerfed, the PHOENIX was almost universally hated for how annoying it was in gameplay, with it's explosive bullets and easy use easily causing chaos in battlegrounds. The general playstyle you will see most with this weapon is largely similar to the CASTIGATE, but with more swordplay, due to the low maximum bullets and high HEAT requirement.
The SIEGE is the third, and most unremarkable weapon in the game. It has a similar appearance to a double-barrelled shotgun. It can be bought for 2,000,000 yen or an unknown amount of crimson credits. The SIEGE's stats are:
  • 30-70 damage per shot, depending on proximity
  • 2 bullets maximum loaded at once
  • Bullet cost: 120H
  • Draw time: 1.1s
  • Special ability: spread shot
The SIEGE is a shotgun, where each individual bullet holds two shots, which each do 30-70 damage depending on how close you are to your opponent, due to spread. The SIEGE has a slightly lower bullet cost than the PHOENIX, but a significantly higher draw time than the PHOENIX and the CASTIGATE. These stats actually make the weapon somewhat skilled, requiring positioning, prediction and knowledge of its high self-knockback to utilise properly. This "skilled" tag however comes from the fact that it is so unremarkable and mediocre to the point where every other weapon can achieve a similar level of effectiveness with significantly lower skill. Outside of battlegrounds, it is never recommended to use this weapon.
The MONARCH is the fourth gun, boasting a modest price of 8,000,000 yen or 2,000 crimson credits. It is best described a brute force killer. The MONARCH's stats are:
  • 140 damage
  • 1 bullet maximum loaded at once
  • Bullet cost: 200H
  • Draw time: 1.85 seconds
  • Special ability: infinite wallbang
The MONARCH is a devastating weapon; with its extreme damage, bullet cost and draw time, and with a single bullet, firing this weapon is an event in itself, the definition of "High risk, High reward". Its special ability lets it shoot through any amount of wall, with some damage drop, but is insignificant in most scenarios. This weapon promotes almost exclusive swordplay, as destabilising your opponent causes them to have a glowing aura that is visible through walls, falling perfectly in tandem with the special ability. If you do however manage to land a shot with this beast of a weapon, there will be intense screen effects and impact frames. If you know how to use this weapon properly, the battleground is yours.
