The basics of playing
REDLINER is a fast paced swordplay / gunplay game on ROBLOX. The core mechanics revolve around gaining speed through various methods and maintaining it to gain the advantage with both your sword and gun.

This wiki covers most of everything you need to know to begin playing REDLINER, from each weapon's statistics to the basic movement, gunplay and swordplay techniques that will assist you in gameplay.
KEYBINDS

The keybinds are:
  • [LMB] (mb1) to attack with sword
  • [RMB] (mb2) to use grappling hook
  • [Shift] to dash in the direction you are moving
  • [Q] to use your gun (as long as you have accumulated enough HEAT, which is explained in weapons)
  • [F] to parry
  • [Ctrl] to slide while moving or to crouch while standing still
  • [Space] while parallel to a wall to wallrun, otherwise used to jump
